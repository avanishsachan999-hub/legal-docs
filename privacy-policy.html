Arrow Escape ("we", "our", or "us") is a standalone logic puzzle game designed to respect your privacy. We are committed to being completely transparent about how the application operates. 2.2 Device & Hardware Permissions

  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used solely to provide optional haptic tactile feedback when arrows escape or encounter a blocked path. Haptic feedback can be toggled on or off at any time in the app settings.
  • No Location Access: The app does not request or access GPS, Wi-Fi location, or cell network location.
  • No Media or Storage Permissions: The app does not read or write external storage, photos, videos, or audio files.
  • No Camera or Microphone Access: The app does not request camera or microphone permissions.

2.3 Local Game Data Storage

All gameplay progress, including unlocked levels, total points, streak count, sound effects preferences, and selected color themes, is stored strictly on your local device via Android's local SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device. You can clear this data at any time through Android System Settings > Apps > Arrow Escape > Storage > Clear Storage.

Arrow Escape does NOT integrate third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking platforms, or data brokers. There are no ads, trackers, or hidden analytics SDKs in the application.
